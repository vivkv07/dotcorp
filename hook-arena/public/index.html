Stop guessing which headline wins.

Paste 3 to 20 headlines, subject lines or hooks. Every pair goes head to head, in both orders, judged by Jev, a decision model that returns calibrated probabilities instead of opinions. You get a ranking, a win margin, and a clickbait flag per line. Usually under three seconds.

    How it works

    1. Every pair, both orders. 10 candidates is 45 pairs and 90 judgments, so position bias cancels out.
    2. Calibrated, not chatty. Jev returns a probability for each side of every matchup, not a paragraph of reasoning.
    3. Bradley-Terry ranking. The same model chess and sports ratings use turns 90 matchups into one ordered list with a win margin.
    4. Clickbait flag. A separate yes/no judgment per line asks whether it overpromises. A winner that also lights up red is a warning.

    Judging is not writing. Use your own voice for the candidates; use the arena to pick.
